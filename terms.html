Terms of use

The short version: Milepost is a record-keeping tool, it isn't tax advice, and the figures are only as good as the data you let it collect.

What Milepost is

An app that records your drives, lets you classify them, and produces a mileage log and a deduction estimate using the published IRS standard mileage rates for the dates travelled.

What it is not

It is not tax advice, accounting advice, or legal advice, and it does not create a professional relationship of any kind. Deductibility depends on your circumstances. You are responsible for what you file, and for checking the figures before you rely on them. If it matters, ask a qualified tax professional.

Accuracy

Milepost measures distance from GPS, which is imperfect. It deliberately discards readings it cannot trust and rounds down. Drives may be missed entirely if location permission is limited, the device is off, or iOS suspends the app. Review your log before relying on it — the app's own report will tell you what looks weak.

Mileage rates

Rates are the IRS standard mileage rates, each carrying the notice it came from. When a year's rate has not been published, Milepost carries the previous year's forward, marks it as unconfirmed, and lets you enter the correct figure. Verify the rate before filing.

Subscriptions

Milepost Pro is an auto-renewing subscription billed through your Apple Account. It renews unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Any unused portion of a free trial is forfeited when you buy a subscription.

Your data

Your data stays on your device. Deleting the app deletes it. Keep an export if you need the record — see the privacy policy.
