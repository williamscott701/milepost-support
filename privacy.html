Privacy policy

Milepost collects nothing. There is no account, no server and no analytics. This page explains what that means in practice.

What Milepost stores, and where

Your trips, routes, named places, vehicles and odometer readings are written to the app's own storage on your device. They are not transmitted anywhere. The developer cannot see them, and there is nothing to request or delete on a server, because no server exists.

Location

Milepost uses your location to detect and measure drives. Location data is processed on your device and stored on your device. It is never uploaded, sold, shared, or used for advertising. You can revoke the permission at any time in iOS Settings; the app keeps working with manually entered trips.

Motion

Milepost reads the motion activity classification iOS already produces (walking, automotive, and so on) to tell driving from other movement. This lets it avoid running GPS continuously. It is used on device and stored only as part of a trip record.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. Milepost receives only whether a subscription is currently active. It never sees your name, payment details or Apple Account.

Third parties

There are none. No analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no advertising identifier, no tracking of any kind.

Children

Milepost is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Backups

If you back up your device to iCloud or a computer, your Milepost data is included in that backup, under Apple's terms rather than these.
